# Zinely — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 30 October 2026**

Zinely is an offline app for making printable zines. This policy covers the Android app, this website,
and feedback you choose to send to Aastra. Aastra is the independent two-person team behind Zinely.

---

## The short version

**The Zinely Android app collects no information from you. It uploads nothing and has no servers.**

Zinely does not ask for internet access. Your zines stay on your phone.

The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash reporting.
Because Zinely does not request internet access, it cannot send your information anywhere, even by accident.

Feedback is separate. If you send a Google Form or an email, the team receives what you choose to
share. You do not need to send feedback to use the app.

---

## What Zinely stores, and where

Zinely keeps its data in its own private storage on your phone:

- **Your zines**: pages, text, layout and settings.
- **Photos you add to a zine**: Zinely keeps a copy of each photo you choose inside its private
  storage. Your zine still opens if you later move or delete the original photo.

No other app can read this private storage. Files leave it only when you save, share, or back up your
work. You do this through Android's system file tools.

## What leaves your device, and only when you ask

Nothing leaves Zinely's private storage unless you ask:

- **Save PDF** writes a PDF of your zine to your **Downloads** folder. There it is an ordinary file on
  your phone, and you control it.
- **Share** gives a PDF to Android's share sheet so you can send it yourself. The app you choose then
  decides what happens to it. Zinely is not involved.
- **Back up** writes a `.zine` backup to the place you choose in Android's file picker. The backup
  stays under your control. You can use it to restore your library later.
- **Restore** reads only the `.zine` backup you select. It adds the valid contents to Zinely's private
  storage on your phone.

Zinely never uploads your work or syncs it automatically. Saving, sharing, backing up, and restoring
happen only when you ask, with files and places you choose.

## Children

The Zinely Android app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has
no account, no profile, and no way to message or talk to other people.

---

## Deleting your data

Delete a zine in the app to remove its private working copy. Uninstall Zinely to remove the app's
private library. PDFs and `.zine` backups that you saved outside the app stay where you put them.
Delete them yourself if you no longer want them.

> **Please note:** Zinely has no cloud backup. Before you uninstall or change phones, make a new
> backup from **Backups**. Keep it somewhere you control. Without a valid backup, uninstalling
> deletes the private library, and Zinely cannot get it back.

## Feedback and email

Feedback is optional. If you use the Google Forms feedback form, the team receives your message and the
time you sent it. If you give them, the team also receives the feedback category and your reply email.
Only the message is required. The form does not ask for your name, file uploads, or automatic app
diagnostics.

If you email **[aritr.g06@gmail.com](mailto:aritr.g06@gmail.com)**, the team receives your message,
your email address, any sender name your email service supplies, and any attachments you choose to
send. Screenshots or short screen recordings can help explain a problem, but you never have to send
them. Please hide private photos, notifications, names, and other personal information first.

The team uses feedback to understand problems, improve Zinely, and reply if you give an email address.
It is not used for marketing and is not sold. Identifiable feedback is kept private. It is not
published in public response summaries or development issues. Development notes may describe the
problem or idea without identifying you.

### Google services

Google Forms hosts the feedback form, and the contact inbox uses Gmail. Google processes what you
submit. It may also process technical information such as your IP address, browser or device
information, and cookies, under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
If you leave the optional email field blank, Google may still process information about your visit.
These services are separate from the offline Android app. The app sends no zines or diagnostics
automatically.

### Retention and deletion

The team deletes identifiable feedback within **90 days of receiving it**. This covers form responses,
feedback emails, attachments, and copies in spreadsheets or downloaded files. Only development notes
that do not identify you may be kept longer. This is a cleanup commitment that the team manages. It is
not an automatic Google Forms deletion setting. Google's privacy policy governs Google's own retention
and deletion.

To ask for earlier deletion of feedback you sent, email
**[aritr.g06@gmail.com](mailto:aritr.g06@gmail.com)**. Give enough detail to find it, such as the
approximate date and topic. If you did not give an email address, we may not be able to identify your
response reliably. Please do not send identity documents or other sensitive information.
