# Zinely 0.9.0-beta.4 — tester package

## Zinely — beta 4

This build lets you replace a photo without rebuilding its frame.

Select a photo in the editor, scroll the action row if needed, and tap **Replace**. Choose another image
from Android's photo picker. The new image keeps the same position, size, rotation, crop, layer, opacity,
photocopier setting, and flip state. **Undo** brings the previous photo back; **Redo** reapplies the new one.

Art has its own **Replace** action. It still opens the Art cabinet rather than the photo picker.

## Before installing

This APK is signed with the same Zinely release key as beta 1–3, so Android can install it as an update
without deleting your zines. **Do not uninstall the existing app.** If a zine matters to you, making a
fresh backup from **Backups** before any beta update is still sensible.

1. Download `zinely-0.9.0-beta.4-release.apk` to the phone.
2. Tap the file. If Android asks whether the app that opened it may install unknown apps, allow that app
   and return to the installer.
3. Choose **Update** / **Install**, then open Zinely normally.

Zinely needs Android 7.0 or newer. It has no account, analytics, cloud sync, or network permission; your
zines stay on the device unless you deliberately export or share a file.

## What to try

1. Replace a photo on the first, middle, and final pages.
2. Cancel once and confirm the original photo and selection stay put.
3. Replace once, then use Undo and Redo.
4. Close and reopen the zine to confirm the replacement persisted.
5. Select an Art piece and confirm its Replace action opens Art, not Photos.

Please send feedback through the same channel that brought you this APK. The most useful report says what
you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead, and your phone/Android version. Screenshots or a
short screen recording are welcome.

## Known limitations

- Android owns the photo picker. On some phones a selected image goes through an extra **Preview** /
  **Done** step before returning to Zinely.
- Replaced and deleted photo assets are not reclaimed yet, because another element or zine may share the
  same content-addressed file. App storage can therefore grow over time.
- Font choice is still unavailable. Unsupported scripts are kept and warned about, but do not print yet.
- Zinely saves the PDF; printing happens in the phone's PDF or print app.
- The public privacy-policy page remains a separate publication gate awaiting owner-provided details. It
  does not change the offline behavior of this APK.
