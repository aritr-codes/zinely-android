# Zinely 0.9.0-beta.9 - tester package

> **Status: released 2026-11-03.** Tag `v0.9.0-beta.9`; GitHub pre-release, APK only.

## Zinely - turn pieces by hand, and small things fixed

This update is about handling pieces on the page, plus a set of small fixes:

- **Turn a piece by hand.** A selected piece has a round knob below it. Drag the knob to turn the piece; it
  snaps to quarter turns. **Arrange > Turn** has a new **Straighten** button.
- **Two fingers anywhere on the page** turn the selected piece, even a small one.
- **Small pieces are easier to handle.** One finger on the middle of a small piece moves it, and a double-tap
  there opens it. A tap just beside a selected small piece picks the piece you tapped, or clears the selection
  on blank paper.
- **Turned text is typed turned.** Editing a turned piece of text shows the words turned, inside the piece.
- **One Undo for repeated taps.** Several Move, Size or Turn taps in a row in Arrange are undone in one step.
- **Small fixes:** a saved PDF is named after the zine; new zines are numbered ("My zine 2", "My zine 3");
  Rename opens with the old name selected and stops at 60 characters; Rename no longer moves a zine to the front
  of My Shelf; Duplicate scrolls to the new copy; the Art sheet keeps Recent and Favourite pieces after the app
  is closed; the keyboard's Enter key starts a new line instead of finishing the edit.
- **Looks:** the ring round a chosen zine on My Shelf is lighter, and the folder sheet's Cancel is a proper
  button beside Make folder.

The full list is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md#090-beta9--2026-11-03--turn-pieces-by-hand-and-small-things-fixed).
Saved zines open as before, and the document, backup and database formats are unchanged.

## Before installing

This APK uses the same Zinely release key as beta.1 through beta.8, so Android can update an existing
release-signed beta without deleting its data. **Do not uninstall the existing app.** Make a fresh backup
from **Backups** before any beta update.

1. Download `zinely-0.9.0-beta.9-release.apk` to the phone.
2. Tap it, allow that file-opening app to install unknown apps if Android asks, then return to the installer.
3. Choose **Update** / **Install**, then open Zinely normally.

### If you have Zinely 0.8.0 or older

Those builds use a different signing key, so Android cannot update them to this beta. Preserve anything
you care about **before** uninstalling the old app: in 0.8.0 use **Preview › → Print setup → Save PDF**;
in 0.6.0-alpha.1, which has no save action, share the zine somewhere durable. Only then uninstall the old
build and install this APK. Uninstalling the old build deletes its private library.

Zinely requires Android 7.0 or newer. It has no account, analytics, cloud sync, or network permission.

### If you might go back to an older version

Nothing new in this release changes what an older version can open: a turn made with the knob is saved as the
same turn Arrange already made. What the [beta.8 notes](0.9.0-beta.8.md#if-you-might-go-back-to-an-older-version)
say still holds. Art Recent and Favourite pieces are not in a backup.

## What to verify

1. Existing zines and backups remain reachable after updating.
2. Select a photo or text: drag the knob below it to turn it; it clicks into place at quarter turns. Undo
   undoes the whole turn.
3. **Arrange > Turn > Straighten** sets a turned piece back upright; it is greyed out when the piece is already
   straight.
4. Put two fingers on the page, away from the selected piece, and twist: the piece turns.
5. Add a narrow piece of text. Drag it by its middle: it moves. Put a second narrow piece beside it, select one,
   tap the other: the tapped one is selected.
6. Turn a text a quarter turn and double-tap it: the words you type stand turned, inside the piece.
7. Save PDF: the file is named after the zine. Make two new zines: they are numbered. Rename one: the old name is
   selected; a typed name stops at 60 characters.
8. Duplicate a zine: My Shelf scrolls to the copy. Close the app and open Art: Recent and Favourites are kept.
9. While typing on the page, Enter starts a new line; Done or Back finishes.
10. Save PDF, Share, Backups, Print & fold, Reframe, Across fold and Read remain unchanged.

## Known limitations

The authoritative list is the changelog's
[Known limitations](../../CHANGELOG.md#090-beta9--2026-11-03--turn-pieces-by-hand-and-small-things-fixed).
In short: no screen-reader (TalkBack) listen has been done for anything new here (deferred by the owner); a second
finger added late, after a long press has cleared the selection, has not been tried; Android 7 to 9 was not tried
on a real phone; over the dark area beside the page the knob's line is hard to see; the knob's touch area takes a
tap meant for what is under it; the first-run hint covers a piece selected near the top of the page until **Got
it**; at twice the normal text size the folder sheet's buttons stack; and everything carried over from beta.8
unless fixed. Google Play distribution is not available; this beta is distributed directly through GitHub as an APK.
