## Zinely — beta 1

Hello, and thank you for trying this.

**Zinely makes a zine.** A zine is a little booklet — eight pages — made from a single sheet of
ordinary paper, folded and cut once. You put photos and words on the pages, print one sheet at home,
fold it, and you have a small book you made.

Zinely works entirely on your phone. **It has no account, no sign-in, and no internet connection of
any kind** — not for saving, not for updates, not for anything. Nothing you put in it leaves your
phone unless you deliberately share a PDF yourself.

This is the first version put in front of anyone outside the project. Some of it is unfinished on
purpose (§4 says exactly what). What I need from you is in §3.

---

## ⚠️ STOP — do you already have Zinely on this phone?

**If yes, do not install this yet. Read §2 first, and do what it says in the order it says.**
Installing this build requires removing the old one, **removing the old one deletes every zine in
it**, and nothing gets it back. Five minutes now or your work is gone.

**If no** — you have never installed Zinely — none of that applies. Go to §1 and enjoy yourself.

---

### 1. Installing it

You will be installing this the manual way — from a file, not from the Play Store. Android calls
that an "unknown source" and will ask you about it once. That prompt is normal and expected here.

1. Download `zinely-0.9.0-beta.1-release.apk` onto the phone.
2. Tap the file. Your phone will say something like *"For your security, your phone isn't allowed to
   install unknown apps from this source."* Tap **Settings**, turn the switch on for whichever app
   you tapped the file from (Files, Chrome, WhatsApp — whatever you used), then come back and tap
   **Install**.
3. Your phone may offer to scan the app before installing. Let it — it will come back clean.
4. Open Zinely. You'll land on an empty shelf with one invitation: **Start a zine**.

It needs Android 7.0 or newer, and about 40 MB of space.

---

### 2. ⚠️ If you already have an older Zinely on this phone

**Read this before you install. It cannot be undone afterwards.**

This build is signed with a different key than every earlier one. Android treats that as a different
app, so it will **refuse** to install over the old one — you will get
`INSTALL_FAILED_UPDATE_INCOMPATIBLE` — and the only way through is to remove the old one first.

**Uninstalling Zinely deletes every zine in it.** There is no backup, no cloud copy, and no restore.
Nothing gets recovered later.

So, in this order:

**1. Open the Zinely you have now and rescue anything you want to keep.** Older versions name these
buttons differently, so work from what you actually see on screen:

- Open a zine and tap **Preview ›** at the top right.
- **If you then see a button called "Print setup", tap it, then tap "Save PDF".** That writes a PDF
  into your phone's **Downloads**, where it survives everything that follows. Repeat for every zine
  you care about — this is the good case.
- **If there is no "Save PDF" anywhere**, you have the very first version I sent, which could only
  share. Work through to the export and **Share** the file somewhere that keeps it: Drive, Files,
  your own email, a chat with yourself. Don't just open it in a viewer — that copy is temporary and
  will not survive the uninstall.
- **Either way**: a PDF is a picture of the finished sheet. You can print it. You cannot edit it
  back into Zinely — the zine itself does not survive.

If you're not sure whether it worked, check your Downloads (or wherever you shared it) and confirm
the file is really there **before** step 2. Once you uninstall, there is no second chance.

**2. Uninstall the old Zinely.** Long-press the icon → App info → Uninstall.

**3. Install this one**, following §1.

**This is the last time you will have to do that.** Every build after this one installs straight over
it and keeps your zines. The break is happening now, once, because the app just got a permanent
identity it did not have before.

### 3. What I'd most like to hear about

Anything at all is welcome. But if you only tell me a few things, make them these:

1. **The first two minutes.** From opening the app to putting the first thing on a page — where did
   you hesitate? What did you expect to happen that didn't? *I want the confusion, not a diagnosis.*
   "I didn't know what to tap" is exactly the useful sentence.
2. **Read.** Tap **Preview ›** at the top right of the editor. It opens **Read** — your pages, one
   at a time, swipe to turn. Does it feel like looking at the thing you made? This screen is new in
   this build and is the reason the build exists. (Yes, the link is still called "Preview" and the
   screen it opens is called "Read". That's on my list. Tell me if it threw you.)
3. **Did you actually print and fold one?** This is the part I can't test for you — your printer,
   your paper, your scissors. Did the pages come out in the right order and the right way up? Print
   at **100% / Actual size**; a printer set to "fit to page" will throw the folding off.
4. **Words that lied to you.** Any button, label, or message that told you one thing and did another.
5. **Anything that felt broken, slow, or ugly.** Including "this looks unfinished" — that's a real
   report.

What I don't need: feature requests for fonts, stickers, or photos spanning two pages. Those are
already planned and already missing on purpose.

---

### 4. What is missing on purpose

None of these are bugs. Reporting them costs you time and tells me nothing new.

**Your work is not backed up.** Your zines live only on this phone, only inside Zinely. Uninstalling
the app, or losing the phone, loses them. Save a PDF of anything you'd be sad to lose.

- **Deleting a photo doesn't free the space back up.** Storage only grows for now.
- **One typeface only.** You can set size, bold, italic, alignment and colour — but not the font.
- **Non-Latin text comes out blank.** Bengali, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and emoji will not
  appear. Only English and other Latin-alphabet text renders. This is a font limitation, and it's a
  real gap that's queued to be fixed.
- **Text styling applies to a whole text box**, not to individual words inside it.
- **Some of the text colours are quite light** on white paper — the teal especially. They are offered
  as-is; pick a darker one for anything you actually need to read.
- **Zinely doesn't print.** It makes the PDF and hands it to your phone; you print from there.
- **The sheet shown under "Print & fold" shows page numbers, not your artwork.** It's a map of where
  each page lands on the paper, not a picture of your zine. **Read** is where you see the zine.
- **On Android 7, 8 and 9**, the first time you save a PDF the phone asks for a storage permission.
  Say yes or the save can't happen. Zinely writes only that one file.

### 5. How to report something

> 🚧 **FOUNDER: replace this block with a real channel before sending.** Suggested: *"Just message me
> — WhatsApp, or <email>. Screenshots very welcome."*

Whatever you send, these four lines make it ten times more useful:

- **What you were doing** — "I was adding a second photo to page 3."
- **What you expected** — "I expected it to sit next to the first one."
- **What happened instead** — "It landed on top of it and I couldn't get it back."
- **Your phone** — make, model, and Android version (Settings → About phone).

A screenshot or a screen recording is worth all four. If the app closes itself, tell me what you did
in the ten seconds before — that's usually enough.

And please say when something worked, or felt nice. It's genuinely as useful as a bug: it tells me
what not to break next.

Thank you. Go make something small and strange.

### Why §2 exists

The signature break is a one-time, unrecoverable data-loss trap, and it is invisible until it fires
— the failure surfaces as an install error, at which point the tester's options are already reduced
to "uninstall and lose everything". It is the only item in the package that must be read *before*
the action it warns about, which is why it sits above the feedback and limitations sections rather
than among them. See [RELEASING.md §3](../RELEASING.md#the-one-time-break-at-090-beta1).
