## Zinely - backups that tell you what happened

This update makes backing up and restoring say plainly what they did. The Backups sheet says when you last
backed up; a backup that fails says where it failed; one zine that can't be read no longer stops the rest; and
restoring adds only the zines that aren't already on your shelf. On the Bench, undo and redo say what they
changed, a tap in the empty part of a window frame or ring reaches what is underneath, the Font button that
did nothing is gone, and with TalkBack a page reads from top to bottom. The full list is in the
[changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md#090-beta6--2026-10-06--backups-that-tell-you-what-happened).
Saved zines and the document format are unchanged.

## Before installing

This APK uses the same Zinely release key as beta.1 through beta.5, so Android can update an existing
release-signed beta without deleting its data. **Do not uninstall the existing app.** Make a fresh backup
from **Backups** before any beta update.

1. Download `zinely-0.9.0-beta.6-release.apk` to the phone.
2. Tap it, allow that file-opening app to install unknown apps if Android asks, then return to the installer.
3. Choose **Update** / **Install**, then open Zinely normally.

### If you have Zinely 0.8.0 or older

Those builds use a different signing key, so Android cannot update them to this beta. Preserve anything
you care about **before** uninstalling the old app: in 0.8.0 use **Preview › → Print setup → Save PDF**;
in 0.6.0-alpha.1, which has no save action, share the zine somewhere durable. Only then uninstall the old
build and install this APK. Uninstalling the old build deletes its private library.

Zinely requires Android 7.0 or newer. It has no account, analytics, cloud sync, or network permission.

## What to verify

1. Existing zines and backups remain reachable after updating.
2. **Backups**: the sheet says when you last backed up, or that you haven't yet. Back up, then restore that
   same file: the result says the zines were already here and adds nothing.
3. On the Bench, change something and press **Undo**: a short line says what was undone. **Redo** says what
   came back.
4. Put a **window frame** (Add → Art) over a photo. Tap the photo through the hole: the photo is selected.
   Tap the frame's drawn band: the frame is selected.
5. Select some words: the toolbar shows Edit, Size, Ink, Duplicate and Delete, and no Font button.
6. Add, Replace, Reframe, Flip, Across fold, Duplicate, Delete, Read, Save PDF, Share and Print & fold remain
   unchanged.

## Known limitations

The authoritative list is the changelog's
[Known limitations](../../CHANGELOG.md#090-beta6--2026-10-06--backups-that-tell-you-what-happened). In short:
tapping through a holed piece has not been checked by ear with TalkBack; a very thin frame over a photo can
take a careful tap, and dragging a selected one from its edge resizes it; a second tap on small selected words
can land on a resize handle; a repeated identical undo line may be spoken only once; earlier versions restore
a partial backup without saying what it left out; transparent pictures show a white box on the cream page
(exports print correctly); on Android 7–9,
"don't ask again" leaves Save PDF on its error until storage is allowed in Settings; replaced photo assets are
retained; with TalkBack, About opens on the Back button; there is no font choice; printing happens in another
app. Google Play distribution is not available; this beta is distributed directly through GitHub as an APK.

### On device - Samsung SM-A176B, Android 16 (API 36), build `A176BXXS7CZH3`, 2026-10-06, this APK

**Install over the existing app.** After the owner reported the shelf backed up, `adb install -r` - no
uninstall - was accepted over `versionCode 10`. `firstInstallTime` stayed `2026-08-31 18:19:22`; the installed
file's SHA-256 matched the `dist/` APK; the app cold-launched in 479 ms and the shelf opened with its zines.

**Smoke pass** (scripted taps at positions taken from the platform accessibility tree; selection read from the
same tree; a throwaway zine made of words and a window frame, with no photo, deleted afterwards):

- **Tap-through, on this minified build with the `e893972` fix.** A window frame over words: a tap in the hole
  on the words selected the words; a tap on the top band and on the left band selected the frame; a tap in the
  hole on blank paper selected the frame; a tap outside the frame selected nothing; a double-tap through the
  hole opened the words for typing.
- **Named undo.** Undo after adding words showed "Words taken off"; Redo showed "Words added".
- **Selected-words toolbar:** Edit, Size, Ink, Duplicate, Delete. No Font button.
- **Save PDF** (Android 16): no permission prompt, "Saved to your phone", exactly one new `zine.pdf` in
  Downloads. The test PDF was deleted.
- **Backup, then restore of the same file.** "Backup saved - All 44 zines are together in one backup file."
  (532,171 bytes). The Backups sheet then read "Last backup saved 6 Oct 2026" with the file's name under it.
  Restoring that file said "Nothing new to add - The zines from this backup are already on your shelf."
- Deleting the throwaway zine returned the shelf to 43 zines. The crash buffer stayed empty.

Not covered by this pass: a photo under a holed piece (the owner's photos were not used), TalkBack, dark
versus light theme, larger text, Reframe, Across fold, Share, and anything on paper.

⚠ **For the owner.** Before this backup the Backups sheet read "Last backup saved 29 Sept 2026", and the only
file dated today in Downloads, `zinely-backup-2026-10-06.zine`, is 54.80 kB against 532 kB for this shelf. That
file is probably not a backup of this shelf. The smoke-pass file `zinely-beta6-smoke-2026-10-06.zine` in
Downloads is a current backup of all 44 zines, including the throwaway one.
