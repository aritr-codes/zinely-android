> **Status: in preparation, 2026-11-09.** Tag `v1.0.0` goes on the merge commit; GitHub release (not a
> pre-release), APK only.

## Zinely 1.0.0 - four paper sizes, paper colour, a third font, and photo descriptions

This is the first full release. You can make a zine, print it, fold it, and keep it safe, all on your phone, with no
account and no network. The version number does not mean every check is done. Known limitations lists what is not.

What is new since 0.9.0-beta.9:

- **Four paper sizes.** Start a zine on A4, US Letter, US Legal or A5. Each row shows the official name as a stamp
  and a plain name: "printer paper, most countries", "printer paper, US and Canada", "long paper", "small paper".
  A US Legal or A5 zine always prints on its own paper.
- **Paper colour.** Each zine remembers the paper you will load: White, Ivory, Pastel yellow, Pastel orange, Pastel
  pink, Pastel purple, Pastel blue, Pastel green, Light brown or Grey. The Bench, the page strip, Read and Print
  details show the page on that paper. Zinely does not print the colour. The PDF has only the ink, so the colour
  comes from your paper.
- **The paper pile.** The colour sheet shows ten small sheets and a sample page. A tap changes the colour and the
  sheet stays open, so you can try each paper. **Done** closes it.
- **Hand.** A third font for the words in your zine, next to Book and Plain.
- **Describe.** Select a photo or an Art piece, tap **Describe**, and type up to 250 characters. Read speaks the page
  for a person who cannot see it. A description is never printed and never in the PDF.
- **Smaller changes.** The "restored details" note on My Shelf lists the zines whose name went back, so none is
  hidden. A zine that cannot be opened keeps its paper size on the Shelf. Undo after choosing several zines no
  longer restores a zine that was deleted meanwhile. The first-run hint no longer covers its piece at large text.
  TalkBack says "button" on the Proof screen's buttons. The full list is in the
  [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md#100--2026-11-09--four-paper-sizes-paper-colour-a-third-font-and-photo-descriptions).

## Before installing

This APK uses the same Zinely release key as beta.1 through beta.9, so Android can update an existing
release-signed beta without deleting its data. **Do not uninstall the existing app.** Make a fresh backup from
**Backups** before you update.

1. Download `zinely-1.0.0-release.apk` to the phone.
2. Tap it, allow that file-opening app to install unknown apps if Android asks, then return to the installer.
3. Choose **Update** or **Install**, then open Zinely.

### If you have Zinely 0.8.0 or older

Those builds use a different signing key, so Android cannot update them. Keep what you care about **before** you
uninstall: in 0.8.0 use **Preview > Print setup > Save PDF**. Only then uninstall the old build and install this
APK. Uninstalling the old build deletes its private library.

Zinely needs Android 7.0 or newer. It has no account, no analytics, no cloud sync and no network permission.

### If you might go back to an older version

- A zine saved on US Legal or A5 paper, or with a photo or Art description, is saved in a newer file format. An older
  Zinely cannot open that zine from a backup. It says the backup needs a newer Zinely and restores nothing from it.
  A zine on A4 or US Letter with no description is saved as before.
- An older Zinely does not keep a zine's paper colour. A zine made with Hand shows in a plainer font on an older
  version. Your words and your choice are kept, and Hand comes back on this version.
- Descriptions and the paper colour are not in the PDF or PNG.

## What to verify

1. Existing zines and backups are still on My Shelf after the update.
2. **Make a zine** shows four papers. Each row says the official name and the plain name. Pick A4.
3. In a zine's menu, open **Paper colour**. Tap Pastel orange: the sample page changes and the sheet stays open.
   Tap **Done**. The menu row and the Bench page now say Pastel orange.
4. Open **Print details**. It says "Put pastel orange paper in your printer. The PDF has only the ink, so the colour
   comes from your paper."
5. Make a zine on US Legal. Print details shows its paper as a fact, with no A4 or US Letter switch.
6. Select some text and set the font to Hand.
7. Select a photo, tap **Describe**, type some words, and open Read: it speaks the words.
8. Save PDF, Share, Backups, Print and fold, Reframe and Read work as before.

## Known limitations

The authoritative list is the changelog's
[Known limitations](../../CHANGELOG.md#100--2026-11-09--four-paper-sizes-paper-colour-a-third-font-and-photo-descriptions).
In short:

- **No TalkBack listen** has been done for the Start sheet, the paper colour sheet, Hand, Describe or Read's new
  stops. A person who uses TalkBack may find wording or order we did not catch.
- **Coloured paper has not been printed on** and compared with the screen. The screen colours are close estimates.
- **The paper colour sheet scrolls about 16 dp** on a phone that is 360 dp wide and 640 dp tall. On a taller phone it
  does not scroll at normal text size ([issue #204](https://github.com/aritr-codes/zinely-android/issues/204)).
- **The paper colour sheet does not close with a swipe down.** No Zinely sheet does. Use Done, Back or a tap outside.
- **Android 7 to 9** has been tried on an emulator, not on a real phone.
- **Six decisions in this release are still Proposed**, not Accepted: ADR-147 (Describe), ADR-148 (Hand), ADR-149,
  ADR-150 (paper sizes and colour), ADR-151 (plain English) and ADR-152 (paper names and the pile). The features ship
  built and checked as the sections below say. ADR-142 (choosing several zines), which shipped in beta.8, is also
  still Proposed.
- Google Play is not available. This release is a direct APK download from GitHub.
