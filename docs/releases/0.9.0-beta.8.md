# Zinely 0.9.0-beta.8 - tester package

> **Status: released 2026-10-29.** Tag `v0.9.0-beta.8` on merge `029d452`; GitHub pre-release, APK only.

## Zinely - choose several zines, mix your own ink, 124 Art pieces, and work that stays safe

This update adds five things you can see and one you hope never to need:

- **My Shelf: choose several zines.** Long press a zine, or tap **Select several** in a zine's menu, tick the
  zines you want, then **Move to a folder** or **Delete**. One Undo puts back every zine that moved or was
  deleted. The Bench still selects one thing at a time.
- **Ink: mix your own.** In the Ink tray, **Mix** opens a colour mixer (a square, three sliders and a hex code).
  **My inks** keeps the last 9 you used; **My palettes** keeps up to 9 named sets of 2 to 6 inks.
- **Art: 124 pieces**, 92 of them new since beta.7 (90 new drawings and two hand-cut frames, below) and 40 of them
  open-source drawings credited in Licences & credits. Zines you already made keep their layout. 56 names were
  shortened so the whole name shows on its tile.
- **Two hand-cut frames, Snipped border and Round window.** A tap in the hole reaches the photo or words under
  it. A thin Snipped border can be hard to pick back up over a photo.
- **Selecting something no longer shrinks the page.** The move, size, turn and layer buttons now sit behind one
  **Arrange** button ([ADR-129](../DECISIONS.md#adr-129), which reverses the owner's earlier OD-11 ruling; the
  owner can overrule it).
- **Never lose work, never get stuck.** Back while typing keeps your words, a message's Undo disappears once you
  make another change, a failed Save PDF says why and that your zine is safe, and My Shelf tells you once when a
  zine's pages or details went back to an earlier copy.

The full list is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md#090-beta8--2026-10-29--choose-several-zines-mix-your-own-ink-124-art-pieces-and-work-that-stays-safe).
Saved zines open as before, and the document and backup formats are unchanged.

These ship **built and merged, not yet fully accepted**: [ADR-138](../DECISIONS.md#adr-138),
[ADR-139](../DECISIONS.md#adr-139), [ADR-140](../DECISIONS.md#adr-140) and

under the owner's delegation, with its device checks still owed and its page-repair note in ADR-140. What is still owed is under [Not verified](#not-verified).

## Before installing

This APK uses the same Zinely release key as beta.1 through beta.7, so Android can update an existing
release-signed beta without deleting its data. **Do not uninstall the existing app.** Make a fresh backup
from **Backups** before any beta update.

1. Download `zinely-0.9.0-beta.8-release.apk` to the phone.
2. Tap it, allow that file-opening app to install unknown apps if Android asks, then return to the installer.
3. Choose **Update** / **Install**, then open Zinely normally.

### If you have Zinely 0.8.0 or older

Those builds use a different signing key, so Android cannot update them to this beta. Preserve anything
you care about **before** uninstalling the old app: in 0.8.0 use **Preview › → Print setup → Save PDF**;
in 0.6.0-alpha.1, which has no save action, share the zine somewhere durable. Only then uninstall the old
build and install this APK. Uninstalling the old build deletes its private library.

Zinely requires Android 7.0 or newer. It has no account, analytics, cloud sync, or network permission.

### If you might go back to an older version

A zine that uses one of the 92 Art pieces new since beta.7 (the 90 drawings or the two frames), opened or
restored on 0.9.0-beta.7 or earlier, is opened with that piece drawn as nothing; an invisible frame can still be
selected where it sits and takes a tap meant for what is under it. A zine with **Book** text, opened on 0.9.0-beta.6 or earlier, is drawn in Plain
(see the [beta.7 notes](0.9.0-beta.7.md#if-you-might-go-back-to-an-older-version)). My inks and My palettes are
not in a backup.

## What to verify

1. Existing zines and backups remain reachable after updating.
2. On My Shelf, long press a zine: it is chosen and a bar shows how many are chosen. Choose two more, tap
   **Move to a folder**, pick or make a folder, and see all three move. Tap **Undo** within a few seconds: all
   three come back.
3. Choose two zines and tap **Delete**, then **Undo**: both return. Choose one zine only: Move and Delete
   speak and behave as for a single zine.
4. Delete two zines, let the Undo time run out, then try Undo again: a message names the zines already
   deleted. Nothing else is touched.
5. Select a piece, open **Ink**, tap **Mix**, set a colour by the square, sliders or hex, tap **Use**: the piece
   takes it and it appears under My inks. Make a palette, edit it, delete it and Undo.
6. Open **Art**: 124 pieces; search finds a piece by its old name (for example "Torn hole"). Add **Round
   window** over a photo: a tap in the hole reaches the photo; a tap on the paper selects the frame.
   Add **Snipped border** over a photo and see whether it can be picked back up.
7. Type, press Back: the words are kept. Empty a box and press Back: the box goes, with Undo.
8. Select a piece: the move, size, turn and layer buttons are behind **Arrange**, and the page keeps its size.
9. Save PDF, Share, Open PDF, Backups, Print & fold, Reframe, Across fold and Read remain unchanged.
10. **Print one page with a new Art piece and say whether it reads well on paper.** Nobody has done this yet.

## Known limitations

The authoritative list is the changelog's
[Known limitations](../../CHANGELOG.md#090-beta8--2026-10-29--choose-several-zines-mix-your-own-ink-124-art-pieces-and-work-that-stays-safe).
In short: no screen-reader (TalkBack) listen has been done for Ink, Art, never-lose-work, choosing several
zines, folders or frames (deferred by the owner); no Art page has been printed; Ink is not yet compared with the
design at 390dp (choosing several zines was, on an emulator, 2026-10-29: PASS; real-phone parity is still owed); Android 7 to 9 was not tried on a real phone; at twice the normal text size the "+ New palette"
pill wraps; at the largest text on a small phone the first-run hint covers the piece it asks you to drag and the
low-storage notice can run taller than the screen; a restore from a page file's `.bak` copy is silent and loses
the last autosave; a repaired zine can carry an invisible empty text box; an Undo racing an in-flight delete can
leave a card restored then deleted with no message; Ink's mixer header names the built-in Forest by its colour
word ("dark green-grey") where the tray calls it Forest (found 2026-10-26, no fix found in the history); the
selection action bar clips "Delete" at twice the text size on a 411dp emulator (found 2026-10-26, possibly older
than this release); the Bench-open-then-return repair path and the D-146 save-failure titles have not been
looked at on a device; and everything carried over from beta.7. Google Play
distribution is not available; this beta is distributed directly through GitHub as an APK.
