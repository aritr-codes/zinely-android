A55 · Plain paper names

A55, frozen 2026-11-05 under the owner's delegation, after three reviews (ADR-152). Amends A35 (v21-library.html) and A35.8 / AW6-P's fixed-paper card (v21-proof.html). The owner asked for paper names that anyone can understand. The print dialog on the phone or the printer still uses the official names ("A4", "Letter", "Legal", "A5"). The maker must find that same name in the print dialog. Thus the official name stays first, as a stamp, and a plain name follows it. Each plain name adds a fact: where people use the paper, or how it is different. "A4: printer paper, most countries", "US Letter: printer paper, US and Canada", "US Legal: long paper", "A5: small paper". A4 and US Letter both have a place in their name, so neither paper is "the normal one" (A35.3). Rev. 3 includes the fixes from three reviews. Each row keeps the zine's page size and the facts that R29.6 asks for. TalkBack says the units in full. The official name stays the key in the code. The A35 notes and spoken labels (Copy.Paper.*_NOTE, *_SPOKEN) and the Print details sentence (printsOnlyOn) do change, with their tests.

A55.6 Print details, a US Legal or A5 zine
The fixed-paper card (A35.8) shows the stamp, the plain name, the sheet size and: "You made this zine for X paper. Use X paper only. Put X paper in your printer before you print." It is still one node with no switch. The A4 / US Letter switch in Print details does not change.
A55.7 Where the plain name does not appear
The Shelf line, the zine sheet line, the Proof band, the Colophon, the PDF and its file name. These keep the official name only.
A55.8 Fit
At font scale 1 the whole sheet fits on a 360 × 640 dp phone with no scrolling (toggle "small phone"). At font scale 2 the rows grow and the sheet scrolls; nothing is cut off; the stamp and the plain name wrap onto two lines.
A55.9 What leaves the row
The A35 country lines and the "wider than US Letter" phrase. The plain name now says where A4 and US Letter are used (the US Letter name undersells its reach into Latin America and the Philippines, which R29.5 calls the safe direction), and the page sizes on line 2 show that a US Legal page is wider.

Decisions in rev. 2. (1) The official name is first, on the screen and in speech. It is the only word that is different for A4 and US Letter, and it is the word in the print dialog. (2) The plain name says a fact: where people use the paper (A4, US Letter), or how it is different (US Legal is long, A5 is small). Rev. 2 said "half-size paper" for A5; in the US "half size" means half of a Letter sheet (5.5 × 8.5 in), which is not A5, so rev. 3 says "small paper". (3) The sheet size and the page size stay on every row, on one line. (4) The sheet fits a small phone with no scrolling (owner, 2026-11-05: "I do not want a scroller"), so the country line and the "wider" phrase left the rows (A55.9). (5) The order rule from A35 does not change: the preferred paper is first. (6) The plain name is shown only where a maker chooses a paper (Start) or prints it (Print details). The Colophon does not change. The Shelf line, the zine sheet line and the band stay as they are, so zineShelfStampLabel and its "·" split do not change. (7) The stamp in a control is square, not tilted (F.9).
