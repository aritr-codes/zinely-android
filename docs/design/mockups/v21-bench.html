A41 · your words and your undo stay yours

Try each state on the phone above. "Back" is Escape on a keyboard, or the button below.

The states, in order:

  1. Back while typing: the words are kept and the piece stays selected (A41.1).
  2. Back on an emptied box: the box is removed, with a Removed snack whose Undo brings it back with its words.
  3. Back on a fresh blank box: the box goes, with no snack and no undo step.
  4. Undo retires: with the Removed snack up, make another edit (or press Undo on the bar) and the snack goes at once (A41.2, D-141).
  5. Photo pick fails: the snack shows the failure line with no button (A41.3).
  6. Reframe fails: “This photo can’t be reframed. Try Replace.” (A41.4).
  7. The Add pill grows with large text (A41.5); at 1x it looks as before.

A40 · Mix an ink

Owner ruling 2026-10-22: makers may mix their own ink with a hex picker, and keep up to 9 in My inks for every zine. Owner looked at the prototype and ruled the cap of 9 the same day. Open the working prototype: v21-ink-mixer.html (five states, dark, largest text, 360dp).

  1. Ink tray: one band added, My inks, after Neutrals and before Starting palettes, for text and Art. It ends with a dashed Mix pot ("Mix an ink"). Band label counts "N of 9" once one is kept.
  2. Mixer sheet opens on the selected piece's current ink: title + colour name; a full-cap note (only when 9 are kept and this code is not one of them); a paper preview (title, a body line and a mark on white, "On white paper · about how it prints"); a fixed-height legibility line; the hex field; one shade square; one hue strip; Cancel and Use this ink pinned.
  3. Hex: strip whitespace, zero-width characters and # before any length cap; only 6 hex digits move the colour live; 3 digits count on Done or leaving the field; otherwise "Still showing #RRGGBB." Use settles the field first. Stored upper-case RRGGBB, no alpha.
  4. Legibility: contrast against #FFFFFF below 3.0:1 shows "Pale in print", worded for text or for Art; never blocks (D-028). Otherwise "Reads clearly on white paper." Same slot, same height.
  5. My inks: app-wide, cap 9 (9 + Mix = two rows of five). Display newest-made first, stable. At the cap the least-recently-used ink is removed and the sheet names it (pot + colour name) before Use, with "Pages already using it keep it." Reuse never duplicates. Undo after Use restores the page's ink and My inks. Long-press (TalkBack custom action) "Remove from My inks", undoable.
  6. TalkBack: kept pot "My ink, <name>, <digits spoken singly>"; Mix "Mix an ink"; the square is touch-only, with two one-axis sliders Colour strength and Lightness (5% steps) and the Hue slider (5°, value = hue name); no live region on the heading name; hex error announced once; toast says the name. Names come from the prototype's colourName and its 38-colour table, which the app's test copies.
  7. Largest text: A40 defines the large-text Ink tray (three pots a row, 72dp); the mixer is one scrolling column, buttons stacked and pinned, square 120dp, type never shrinks.

A40 rev. 2 · Palettes

Owner asked 2026-10-22 for named palettes and a tray that puts the maker's own work first; looked twice ("trim the text, avoid the scroller"; palette cap 9, not 12) and said "freeze". Prototype: v21-ink-mixer.html (eight states, dark, largest text, 360dp).

  1. Tray order: Current ink → My palettes → My inks → Built-in inks → Starter palettes (closed disclosure, "3 to copy"). Replaces rule 1's band order.
  2. Built-in inks: the 13 frozen Spot inks and Neutrals (Ink once) in one 7-across grid, 28dp pots in 48dp targets, no visible names (Current ink shows the picked name; TalkBack reads every name). Paper tints follow for Art only.
  3. My palettes: up to 9, newest first, never evicted; a 10th (New, Duplicate, Save a copy) asks to delete one. Each holds 2–6 inks as copies of values, not links. One palette is open (name, ⋯, its pots); every palette also shows as a colour strip in a radio group, five to a row; the last opened stays open. Tap a pot to use that ink: a palette never recolours a page. ⋯ = Edit, Duplicate, Delete, each undoable.
  4. New / Edit palette in the tray: name field (unique default "My palette N", duplicates allowed and noted, not autofocused), strip of chosen inks (tap to remove), every ink as an aria-pressed toggle with a ✓ badge, "Was in this palette" row to put a removed ink back, Mix adds to the palette and My inks (a full palette says "Save to My inks"). New starts with the current ink. Save needs a name and 2 inks. Cancel or Back with changes: "Palette not saved · Undo".
  5. Mixer, trimmed: the legibility line is the paper preview's footer ("Reads clearly on white paper" / "Pale in print. Words may be hard to read." / Art: "Fine for a soft mark or a tint."); the preview has no body line; hex help is a placeholder and appears only for an error; full note: "My inks is full. This replaces <name>, used longest ago. Your pages keep it." Fits without scrolling at default text; scrolls at the largest.
  6. TalkBack: the current ink reads ", selected"; palette pots "<name>, from <palette>"; strips "<palette>, N inks" as radios; focus stays on the control after any re-render.
