What this prototype is proving

  • 1Edit where it lives. Double-tap the title: the caret appears in the real text, the whole page pans up as one rigid body to clear the keyboard, and “Done” settles it back to the identical spot. Steadiness and locality — the fork the owner ruled for.
  • 2The page is the hero. Chrome recedes; selecting one element quietly dims the rest; contextual tools appear near the work and never sit on top of it.
  • 3Print-correctness, felt. The soft keep-clear inset is the only “margin” a beginner ever meets — no one learns the word “bleed.”
  • 4Assets in three layers. Add → Assets shows bundled items (offline, no badge); “Search online” is off until you opt in, and then it sends only a keyword — never your zine.
  • 5Reversible by default. Delete soft-deletes with a visible Undo, not a modal. Autosave is silent but shown (“Saved · on this device”).
