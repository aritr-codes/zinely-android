What this studio is proving

  • EDITEdit where it lives. Double-tap the title: caret in the real text, the whole page pans up as one rigid body, and “Done” settles it back pixel-identical. Steadiness and locality.
  • H1A shelf, not just a page. Gather photos and bits on “Your shelf,” then tap one to lay it on the page. Collect now, arrange later — the heart of a studio.
  • H2Scales 1 → 32 pages. Few pages show as dots; more become a scrolling filmstrip; the ⊞ opens the whole zine as a page grid. One nav, three shapes.
  • H3One calm “Art” drawer. Add stays three verbs; Art is one searchable surface with chips + Recent + ⭐. “Search online” is the single opt-in step — only a keyword ever leaves the phone.
  • H4Colour to make with. A maker “ink” palette (riso spot-inks, named, in bands) lives in the Ink picker — separate from the app’s own restrained colours. Edit a title → tap Ink.
  • SAFEReversible by default. Delete soft-deletes with a visible Undo, no modal. Alignment guides appear only while you work; autosave stays a quiet “Saved.”
