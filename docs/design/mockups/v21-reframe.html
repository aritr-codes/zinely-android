What changes is that the room stops changing when you enter it.

What the re-skin changes, and why each one.

1. Coral is gone. V1's coralStrong carried Done and the selected fit segment. V2.1 has no coral; its primary is --leaf under --on-leaf, and this surface now says so.
2. Two floating surfaces became one room. The session used a --menu pill floating over a --desk toolbar — two grounds the rest of V2.1 does not have. The pad is now a --paper card on the bench and the band is the Bench's own .bar, so entering a session changes the tools and not the room.
3. The fit choice is two chips, not a segmented control. It is one binary; the corpus already has a two-state chip and it costs less than a segment pair. The wording stays in outcome language.
4. Done wears .add's clothes, ring included. One ring per screen: .add is not on screen during a session, Done stands where it stood and is the primary. The ring moves with primacy rather than multiplying.
5. The boundary says what it means. "Anything past this line gets trimmed off" — never bleed, trim area, safe area or crop marks, which printers themselves document as the terms that confuse beginners most.

What this file deliberately does NOT change. The pad stays visible. Inside a session the photo is moved by dragging and scaled by pinching, and nothing registers a custom accessibility action for either — so the arrows and the ± steppers are the single-pointer alternative WCAG 2.5.1 and 2.5.7 require, and the W3C's own worked example for both is a pan/zoom surface with exactly these buttons. Hiding them behind a disclosure would be removing a conformance mechanism and calling it tidying. If a future pass wants the room quieter, the nudge and zoom verbs must exist as custom actions on the photo first.

One question this file does not answer, because it is the owner's. "Reframe" is a designer's word. The user holds the question "how much of my photo will show?", and the research brief argues for Move photo or Fit photo instead. Renaming a shipped verb touches the context bar, the chip, the custom accessibility action and its copy tests, so it is proposed here and not taken.
