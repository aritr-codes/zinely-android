A56 · Paper colour, as a pile of paper

A56, frozen 2026-11-05 under the owner's delegation, after three reviews (ADR-152). Amends AW6.4, AW6.5, AW6.11, AW6.12 (v21-library.html) and AW6-P.3 (v21-proof.html). The chooser opens from the zine sheet and from Print details, as before. The owner said that the chooser "looks good but something is missing", and later: "I do not want a scroller". This design makes three changes. The colours are sheets of paper on the desk, not chips in a settings list. A sample page shows the inks on the chosen paper; a tap changes the colour at once and the sheet stays open, so the maker can try each paper. And the whole sheet fits a small phone with no scrolling. The sample page is labelled as a sample. It is not the maker's cover, because a cover is a recipe and is never a rendered page (ADR-069, AW6.7), and the Shelf has no rendered page.

Before (AW6)

Paper colour

A56: the paper pile

Paper colour

Choose the paper that you will put in your printer. Zinely shows the colour on the screen only. The PDF has only the ink.

Home printers have no white ink, so this list has no dark or bright paper. The colours on the screen are approximately correct.

A56.1 The chooser, top to bottom
Grab handle; title "Paper colour"; the sub-line; the top row (the sample page, then its label above Done); the pile; the foot line. Words: the sub-line and foot as drawn here.
A56.2 Fit
At font scale 1 the whole sheet fits on a 360 × 640 dp phone with no scrolling (toggle "small phone"; the line above the phones measures it). At font scale 2 the sheet scrolls and the top row is pinned under the title, so the sample page and Done never leave the screen. No scroll bar is drawn.
A56.3 The pile
Nine sheets, two rows of five and four (three columns at font scale 2), in this order: White, Ivory, Pastel yellow, Pastel orange, Pastel pink, Pastel purple, Pastel blue, Pastel green, Grey. A sheet is 42 × 58 dp (52 × 72 at font scale 2), a 1.5dp ink edge and a 3dp hard shadow; its whole cell (at least 48 dp) is the target. At rest each sheet tilts by its own fixed angle (the list in this script, never re-rolled). The name is under the sheet.
A56.4 The chosen sheet
Square (no tilt), a piece of tape across its top, and the word "Chosen" in ink with a 3dp berry rule under it. Never colour alone.
A56.5 A tap
Changes the zine's paper colour at once (saved like any pick) and the sheet stays open. The sample page and its label change at once, with no fade. Done, Back, the scrim and a swipe down close the sheet; focus goes back to the row that opened it (the zine sheet's or Print details' Paper colour row, AW6.12). No confirm step and no Undo snack: the change prints nothing and the same sheet puts it back.
A56.6 The sample page and its label
AW6's stand-in page, the same at both doors, square. The label: "A sample page on [name] paper", the name lower-case (AW6.13); for White, "A sample page on white paper, plain printer paper". Both are hidden from TalkBack: the checked radio button already says the choice.
A56.7 TalkBack
"Paper colour" (dialog), the sub-line, "Done" (button), then each sheet as a radio button "[name]" (White: "White. Plain printer paper."), checked or not checked, "n of 9", then the foot line.
A56.8 Two new papers
Pastel orange (id pastel-orange, #F5CFA8) and Pastel purple (id pastel-purple, #DCD0EC), estimates like every R41 value (R41.7), both through the AW6.6 gate below. Add-only: no schema change. An older build reads an unknown id as White and keeps the id in the file.
A56.9 Print details' note (amends AW6-P.3)
Under the colour row, for a colour that is not White: "Put [name] paper in your printer. The PDF has only the ink, so the colour comes from your paper." The name is lower-case. "Load" leaves the app: it is printer jargon.
A56.10 Not changed
Where the chooser opens, the radiogroup, the legibility gate, the paper layer (AW6-B, AW6-P), "Your paper colour is not in the file.", and the zine sheet row.

Both values are estimates, as every R41 value is: no maker publishes the colour of its paper (R41.7). Pastel orange is R41's "Apricot" value. The names follow AW6.5: "Pastel" is how shops sell coloured copy paper. The colour words "orange" and "purple" also name the hue of some inks (Ochre, Plum), as "pink" and "blue" do; the word "Pastel" keeps the paper names apart from them. Order in the pile: warm to cool, then Grey, as a paper pack is sorted. The stored ids of the seven shipped papers do not change.
