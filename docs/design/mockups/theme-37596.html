What does not move with the mood

Paper stays physical.

Pages, thumbnails, fold sheets, previews and exports use the document's stock in both themes.

Pink stays punctuation.

Camaron marks current/selected. It never becomes the next-move action or a destructive state.

Contrast is authored.

Dark ink sits on all six source swatches. Night controls use Avocado Cream with olive-black ink.
