The Living Product

"The interface stays quiet. The creations carry the warmth."

Not another principle — the proof. Every cover below is generated from one frozen recipe. The interface never changes; the shelf does all the talking. Watch a collection grow, stress it to fifty books, and ask the only question that matters: does it stay unmistakably Zinely without going repetitive?

01

One recipe, exploded

A Zinely cover is not drawn — it is assembled from five variables on one frozen grid (the Penguin/Marber move). Change any variable, get a new book that still belongs to the family. The grid, the type pairing, the muted inks and the paper warmth are held constant; the meaning lives in the mix.

Held constant (the recognisability): frozen 3:4 grid · Fraunces title / Inter meta · muted riso inks on warm paper · spine · a fixed stamp vocabulary. Honours ADR-069 — recipe-driven, no per-edit render.

05

Why it never drifts — constants vs variables

Recognisability and variety are not in tension when you decide which is which. Zinely fixes the grammar and frees the vocabulary — the opposite of a template, which fixes the vocabulary and frees nothing.

Constant — the Zinely signature

What makes any two covers recognisably siblings.

  • The 3:4 grid & margins — one proportion, always.
  • Fraunces title / Inter meta — one voice pairing.
  • Muted riso inks on warm paper — never full-saturation, never cold white.
  • Spine · stamp · maker's mark — the object cues of a real little book.
  • Texture as printing, not decoration — grain, misregistration, fleck.

Variable — the maker's hand

What makes every cover unmistakably theirs.

  • Title & subtitle — the words are the loudest signal.
  • Ink & second ink — 10 riso spot inks, any pair.
  • Paper stock — cream, kraft, fleck, dot-grid, cover-weight.
  • Motif & stamp — the personal layer, most-used.
  • Layout archetype — classic, centered, banded masthead.
06

Materials, doing the work

The warmth is physical, not stylistic. Every texture here is the believable consequence of a material or a press — never an overlay dropped on for mood. Read each caption: it names the cause.

The texture rule (guardrail)

Grain is the riso drum + paper tooth. Misregistration is two drums, printed in two passes. Fleck is recycled pulp. Dot-grid is a notebook register. If a texture can't name its physical cause, it doesn't ship — that's the line between craft and kitsch, and it holds across the whole content system.

07

Where the colour is allowed to live

The café palette is real — it just isn't in the chrome. The interface spends one accent; the creations spend the whole box. If a screen ever needs more warmth, it comes from content, not chrome.

The interface — one quiet accent

Matcha, for the single action. That is the entire chrome colour budget.

The creations — the whole box
