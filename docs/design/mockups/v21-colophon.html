About Zinely

A little about this little app

We’re Aastra, the two people behind Zinely. We wanted to make little paper books, and somehow that turned into making an Android app.

The page order became our problem. What goes on the pages is entirely yours.

Thanks for making something with it.

Paper for new zines

We’ll suggest this paper when you start. You can always choose the other one.

Your zines stay yours

Zinely works offline. Your zines stay on this device unless you choose to share or back them up.
