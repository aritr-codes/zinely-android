Accessibility

Made to be usable.

The Zinely website is designed and maintained to meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0 Level AA.

Last reviewed: 9 September 2026

What we support

  • Keyboard navigation and a visible keyboard focus indicator.
  • A skip link, meaningful headings, landmarks, and descriptive link text.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images and hidden decorative images.
  • Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 and layouts that accommodate 200% text resizing.
  • Reduced-motion preferences and no audio, autoplay, flashing content, forms, or timed interactions.
