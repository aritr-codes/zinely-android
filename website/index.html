About Zinely

Small enough to make. Real enough to keep.

Zinely is an Android app for making eight-page mini zines from photos, words, and simple art. It began with a wish to make something personal for someone else—without an account, a feed, or sending your work to a cloud.

Make it on your phone. Print one sheet. Cut, fold, and pass it on.

Accessibility
