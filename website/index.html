A tiny print studio for Android

Make a little zine.

Photos, words, and whatever you feel like. Zinely turns them into an eight-page booklet you can print, cut, and fold at home.

Quietly private

Your zines stay yours.

Zinely works offline. There is no sign-in, advertising, tracking, analytics, or cloud upload. Your work stays on your device unless you choose to save, share, or back it up.
