Public beta release

Published 29 October 2026

0.9.0-beta.8

Choose several zines, mix your own ink, 124 pieces of Art, and work that stays safe

Released and in the download now. It installs over earlier betas and keeps your zines. The way zines are saved has not changed. Make a backup first.

Added

  • Choose several zines on your shelf. Press and hold a zine, or tap Select several in its menu, pick more, then move them to a folder or delete them together. One Undo puts them all back.
  • Mix your own ink. In the Ink tray, Mix opens a colour mixer. My inks keeps the last nine you used, and My palettes keeps up to nine named sets of inks.
  • 124 pieces of Art, better drawn, with shorter names so each shows in full on its tile. Two of them are new hand-cut frames, Snipped border and Round window. Zines you already made keep their layout.
  • Your shelf tells you, once, when a zine’s pages or details went back to an earlier copy after a file was damaged.

Changed

  • The buttons that move, size, turn and layer a piece now sit behind one Arrange button, so selecting something no longer shrinks the page.
  • A message’s Undo goes away once you make another change, so it can’t undo the wrong thing.

Fixed

  • Pressing Back while you type keeps your words. The Bench has a button back to your shelf.
  • When Save PDF fails, it says why (out of memory, out of space, or storage permission) and that your zine is safe.
  • At the largest text size, the text controls no longer crowd together, and the first-time tip no longer cuts off its first and last words.
  • A notice no longer says a character “can’t print yet” when your phone’s own font draws it.
  • Undo after a batch delete tells you which zines were already gone, and undoing a batch move leaves alone any zine you deleted in the meantime.

Known limitations

  • We haven’t tested any of this with a screen reader yet, and we haven’t printed a page with the new Art pieces.
  • Mixed inks and palettes aren’t in a backup, so they don’t move to a new phone. At twice the normal text size, the “+ New palette” button wraps onto two lines.
  • At the largest text size on a small phone, the first-time tip can cover the piece it asks you to drag.
  • If a zine’s main file is restored from its backup copy, Zinely doesn’t tell you, and the last few seconds of changes are lost.
  • Older versions of Zinely draw nothing for a new Art piece.

All of them are listed on the download page.

Public beta release

Published 11 October 2026

0.9.0-beta.7

Folders, and a second font

The previous public beta. It installs over earlier betas and keeps your zines. The way zines are saved has not changed.

Added

  • Folders on your shelf. From a zine’s three dots, choose Move to a folder. A folder shows as a pile of its zines’ covers. Tap it to look inside, rename it, or take its zines out. A backup remembers which folder each zine was in.
  • A second font for your words. Select some words, tap Text, and choose Book or Plain. Book is a bookish serif with its own bold and italic. Plain is the font every zine has used so far.
  • While you type, your words are shown in their own font.

Changed

  • The button that opens the text card is now called Text. It used to be called Size.

Fixed

  • A zine you deleted and then restored from a backup could stay hidden until you restarted the app. It now shows up at once and stays.
  • Longer text used to jump when you tapped Done, because the lines sat closer together while you typed. Now it barely moves.

Known limitations

  • Folders and the Book font are new. We haven’t tested them with a screen reader yet, or printed a page to check Book at its smallest size.
  • Words that just fit their box can lose their last line when you switch them to Book. Make the box taller to see it again.
  • Book has no Greek or Cyrillic letters, and you can’t choose it for words smaller than 12 pt. The card tells you why.
  • Older versions of Zinely show Book words in Plain and can drop lines that no longer fit. They also restore a backup with every zine on My Shelf.
  • See-through pictures still come in on white. Printing happens in another app.

All of them are listed on the download page.

Public beta release

Published 6 October 2026

0.9.0-beta.6

Backups that tell you what happened

The previous public beta. It installs over earlier betas and keeps your zines. Your saved zines open exactly as before.

Added

  • Undo and redo say what they did. A short line names the change, like “Words taken off”.
  • You can reach through a frame. Tap the photo or words showing through a window frame, a ring, or a photo corner, and you get what’s underneath. Tap the drawn part to pick the piece up.

Changed

  • Restoring a backup adds only the zines that aren’t already on your shelf. Nothing on the shelf is replaced, and a zine you changed since the backup is added alongside.
  • The Font button is gone from the words toolbar, because it didn’t let you choose a font.

Fixed

  • Backups tells you when you last backed up on this phone, or that you haven’t yet. It no longer says your zines are “kept safe” before a backup has run.
  • A backup that fails says where it failed, and Zinely tries to remove the unfinished file.
  • One zine that can’t be read no longer stops the whole backup. The rest is saved, and the result names what was left out.
  • A restore says what happened: “Not enough space” when the phone is full, and no “cancelled” message once zines have been added.
  • With a screen reader (TalkBack), the page you’re working on reads from top to bottom and left to right, whatever is stacked on top.
  • With a screen reader, the dimmed area behind a zine’s menu on your shelf is no longer read out as a button.

Known limitations

  • We haven’t tested tapping through a frame with a screen reader yet. A thin frame over a photo can take a careful tap, and dragging a selected frame from its edge resizes it; drag from the middle to move it.
  • With a screen reader, an undo message that repeats may be spoken only once.
  • If a backup had to leave a zine out, older versions of Zinely restore that backup without telling you a zine is missing.
  • See-through pictures still come in on white. You could not choose a font yet. Printing happens in another app.

Public beta release

Published 25 September 2026

0.9.0-beta.5

Steadier editing and saving

The previous public beta. It installs over earlier betas and keeps your zines. Your saved zines and backups work exactly as before.

Changed

  • The About screen opens with a short note from Aastra, the two of us who make Zinely. Font credits now sit one tap away under Licences & credits.
  • Moving a photo inside its frame (Reframe) opens big photos more smoothly. Your crop, flip, and printed page still use the full-size photo.

Fixed

  • On Android 7, 8 and 9, Save PDF now asks for permission to save files instead of failing every time. Decline, and nothing is saved. Share never needs it.
  • On those phones, a save that fails part-way no longer leaves an empty or broken file in Downloads.
  • A flipped photo stretched across two facing pages now lands the right way round on both, so the printed pages line up.
  • After an Undo that jumps to another page, nothing from the page you left stays selected.
  • Pictures with see-through backgrounds, like logos, come in on white instead of black.
  • A move or resize that ends exactly where it started no longer clears Redo or adds an Undo that does nothing.
  • With a screen reader, the dimmed area behind most pop-up panels, such as Start a zine and Backups, is no longer read out as a button.

Known limitations

  • See-through pictures don’t stay see-through. They print correctly, but on the cream page in the app a logo shows as a faint white rectangle.
  • On Android 7, 8 and 9, if you say no to saving files and tell Android not to ask again, Save PDF keeps failing until you allow it in your phone’s settings. Share still works.
  • With a screen reader, About and its credits pages start on the Back button, and going back lands at the top of the previous screen. Everything is still reachable by swiping. The dimmed area behind a zine’s menu is still read out as a button here; fixed in beta.6.
  • Replaced and deleted photos still take up space. You cannot choose a font yet. Writing that Zinely cannot print yet, such as Bengali or Tamil, is kept but does not print. Printing happens in another app.
