Public beta release

Published 29 October 2026

0.9.0-beta.8

Select several zines, mix inks, 124 pieces of Art

In the download now. It installs over earlier betas and keeps your zines. Make a backup first.

Added

  • Select several zines at once. Move them to a folder or delete them together. One Undo brings them all back.
  • Mix your own ink. My inks keeps the last nine inks you used. My palettes keeps up to nine named sets.
  • 124 pieces of Art, better drawn, with shorter names. Two are new frames: Snipped border and Round window. Your old zines look the same.
  • A note on your shelf when Zinely repairs a zine’s details from an earlier copy.

Changed

  • One Arrange button holds the move, size, turn and layer buttons. The page no longer shrinks when you select something.
  • The Undo on a message goes away when you make another change.

Fixed

  • Back keeps your words while you type. The Bench has a button back to your shelf.
  • A failed Save PDF now says why. It also says your zine is safe.
  • At the largest text size, the text controls fit. The first-time tip shows all its words.
  • A notice no longer says a character “can’t print yet” when it does print.
  • Undo after you delete several zines names the zines that were already gone. Undoing a move keeps zines you deleted.

Known limitations

  • We have not tested this with a screen reader. We have not printed a page with the new Art.
  • A backup does not include mixed inks or palettes. At twice the normal text size, “+ New palette” wraps onto two lines.
  • At the largest text size on a small phone, the first-time tip can cover the piece you drag.
  • If Zinely restores a zine’s main file from its backup copy, it does not tell you. The last few seconds of changes are lost.
  • Older versions of Zinely draw nothing for a new Art piece.

The download page lists all of them.

Public beta release

Published 11 October 2026

0.9.0-beta.7

Folders and a second font

Earlier public beta. It installs over earlier betas and keeps your zines.

Added

  • Folders on your shelf. Use Move to a folder in a zine’s menu. A backup remembers each zine’s folder.
  • A second font: Book. Select words, tap Text, then choose Book or Plain. Plain is the font every zine used before.
  • Your words show in their own font while you type.

Changed

  • The Size button is now called Text.

Fixed

  • A zine you deleted and then restored from a backup stayed hidden until you restarted the app. It now shows at once.
  • Long text no longer jumps when you tap Done.

Known limitations

  • We have not tested folders or Book with a screen reader. We have not printed a page to check Book at its smallest size.
  • Words that just fit their box can lose their last line when you switch to Book. Make the box taller.
  • Book has no Greek or Cyrillic letters. You cannot choose it for words smaller than 12 pt.
  • Older versions of Zinely show Book words in Plain and can drop lines that do not fit. They restore a backup with every zine on My Shelf.
  • See-through pictures still come in on white. Another app does the printing.

The download page lists all of them.

Public beta release

Published 6 October 2026

0.9.0-beta.6

Clearer backups

Earlier public beta. It installs over earlier betas and keeps your zines.

Added

  • Undo and redo name the change, for example “Words taken off”.
  • Tap through a frame. Tap the empty part of a frame to reach the photo or words under it. Tap the drawn part to pick up the frame.

Changed

  • Restore adds only zines that are not on your shelf. It does not replace anything. A zine you changed after the backup is added next to the old one.
  • Removed the Font button from the words toolbar. It did not choose a font.

Fixed

  • Backups shows when you last backed up, or that you have not. It no longer says “kept safe” before a backup.
  • A failed backup says where it failed. Zinely tries to remove the unfinished file.
  • One unreadable zine no longer stops a backup. The rest is saved. The result names the zine that was left out.
  • A restore says what happened. It says “Not enough space” when the phone is full. It no longer says “cancelled” after zines are added.
  • With TalkBack, the page reads from top to bottom and left to right.
  • With a screen reader, the dim area behind a zine’s menu is no longer read as a button.

Known limitations

  • We have not tested tapping through a frame with a screen reader. A thin frame over a photo can need a careful tap. Dragging a frame by its edge resizes it. Drag from the middle to move it.
  • With a screen reader, an undo message that repeats can be spoken once.
  • If a backup left a zine out, older versions of Zinely do not tell you when you restore it.
  • See-through pictures still come in on white. You cannot choose a font yet. Another app does the printing.

Public beta release

Published 25 September 2026

0.9.0-beta.5

Steadier editing and saving

Earlier public beta. It installs over earlier betas and keeps your zines.

Changed

  • The About screen opens with a note from Aastra, the two of us who make Zinely. Font credits are under Licences & credits.
  • Reframe opens big photos more smoothly. Your crop, flip and printed page still use the full-size photo.

Fixed

  • On Android 7, 8 and 9, Save PDF now asks for permission to save files. Before, it always failed. If you decline, nothing is saved. Share never needs it.
  • On those phones, a failed save no longer leaves a broken file in Downloads.
  • A flipped photo across two facing pages now prints the right way round.
  • After an Undo that goes to another page, nothing from the old page stays selected.
  • Pictures with see-through backgrounds, such as logos, come in on white, not black.
  • A move or resize that ends where it started no longer clears Redo. It no longer adds an Undo that does nothing.
  • With a screen reader, the dim area behind most pop-up panels, such as Start a zine and Backups, is no longer read as a button.

Known limitations

  • See-through pictures do not stay see-through. They print correctly. On the cream page in the app, a logo shows as a faint white rectangle.
  • On Android 7, 8 and 9, if you decline and tell Android not to ask again, Save PDF fails until you allow it in your phone’s settings. Share still works.
  • With a screen reader, About and its credits pages start on the Back button. Going back lands at the top of the previous screen. You can still reach everything by swiping. The dim area behind a zine’s menu is still read as a button here. Fixed in beta.6.
  • Replaced and deleted photos still use space. You cannot choose a font yet. Zinely keeps writing it cannot print yet, such as Bengali or Tamil, but does not print it. Another app does the printing.
