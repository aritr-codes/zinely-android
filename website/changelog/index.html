Public beta release

Published 6 October 2026

0.9.0-beta.6

Backups that tell you what happened

Released and in the download now. It installs over earlier betas and keeps your zines. Your saved zines open exactly as before.

Added

  • Undo and redo say what they did. A short line names the change, like “Words taken off”.
  • You can reach through a frame. Tap the photo or words showing through a window frame, a ring, or a photo corner, and you get what’s underneath. Tap the frame itself to pick it up.

Changed

  • Restoring a backup adds only the zines that aren’t already on your shelf. Nothing on the shelf is replaced, and a zine you changed since the backup is added alongside.
  • The Font button is gone from the words toolbar, because it didn’t let you choose a font.

Fixed

  • Backups tells you when you last backed up on this phone, or that you haven’t yet. It no longer says your zines are “kept safe” before a backup has run.
  • A backup that fails says where it failed, and Zinely tries to remove the unfinished file.
  • One zine that can’t be read no longer stops the whole backup. The rest is saved, and the result names what was left out.
  • A restore says what happened: “Not enough space” when the phone is full, and no “cancelled” message once zines have been added.
  • With a screen reader (TalkBack), the page you’re working on reads from top to bottom and left to right, whatever is stacked on top.
  • With a screen reader, the dimmed area behind a zine’s menu on your shelf is no longer read out as a button.

Known limitations

  • We haven’t tested tapping through a frame with a screen reader yet. A thin frame over a photo can take a careful tap, and dragging a selected frame from its edge resizes it.
  • With a screen reader, an undo message that repeats may be spoken only once.
  • If a backup had to leave a zine out, older versions of Zinely restore it without telling you.
  • See-through pictures still come in on white. You cannot choose a font yet. Printing happens in another app.

All of them are listed on the download page.

Public beta release

Published 25 September 2026

0.9.0-beta.5

Steadier editing and saving

The previous public beta. It installs over earlier betas and keeps your zines. Your saved zines and backups work exactly as before.

Changed

  • The About screen opens with a short note from Aastra, the two of us who make Zinely. Font credits now sit one tap away under Licences & credits.
  • Reframe opens big photos more smoothly. Your crop, flip, and printed page still use the full-size photo.

Fixed

  • On Android 7, 8 and 9, Save PDF now asks for permission to save files instead of failing every time. Decline, and nothing is saved. Share never needs it.
  • On those phones, a save that fails part-way no longer leaves an empty or broken file in Downloads.
  • A flipped photo stretched across two facing pages now lands the right way round on both, so the printed pages line up.
  • After an Undo that jumps to another page, nothing from the page you left stays selected.
  • Pictures with see-through backgrounds, like logos, come in on white instead of black.
  • A move or resize that ends exactly where it started no longer wipes Redo or adds an empty Undo step.
  • With a screen reader, the dimmed area behind most pop-up panels, such as Start a zine and Backups, is no longer read out as a button.

Known limitations

  • See-through pictures lose their transparency. They print correctly, but on the cream page in the app a logo shows as a faint white rectangle.
  • On Android 7, 8 and 9, if you say no to saving files and tell Android not to ask again, Save PDF keeps failing until you allow it in your phone’s settings. Share still works.
  • With a screen reader, About and its credits pages start on the Back button, and going back lands at the top of the previous screen. Everything is still reachable by swiping. The dimmed area behind a zine’s menu was still read out as a button.
  • Replaced and deleted photos still take up space. You cannot choose a font yet. Writing in alphabets Zinely cannot print yet, such as Bengali or Tamil, is kept but does not print. Printing happens in another app.
