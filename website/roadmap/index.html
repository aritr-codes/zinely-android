Foundation

Getting Zinely, keeping your work

The ground under the desk

  • Available Works offline with no account. Back up your whole shelf to a file and restore it later.
  • In development The next beta, with a round of fixes. It is being tested and is not in the download yet.
  • In development A short note from the two of us in the app’s About screen. Built, still being checked on a real phone.
  • Planned Zinely on Google Play. Account approval, testing, and store review still have to happen. Until then, download the Android beta here.

Craft

Making the pages

On the bench

  • Available Photos, words, and Art. Reframe, flip, copy, the photocopier look, undo and redo.
  • In development A tidier text toolbar. We’re checking a version without the Font button, because it doesn’t let you choose a font yet. This is still being tested, not in the download.
  • Exploring Font choices, more Art, frames, see-through photos, and new ways to crop or cut out photos. These are ideas under consideration, not announced features.

Proof

Reading, printing, folding

From screen to paper

  • Available Read the finished zine page by page. Share a print-ready A4 or US Letter PDF, or save it to Downloads on Android 10 and newer. Follow the fold guide in the app or on this site.
  • Exploring Fewer “wait, which way?” moments. We want to watch first-time makers use the fold guide with real paper, then improve the steps that make them pause.

Not on our list

Some things are off the table on purpose, so you never have to wonder:

  • Accounts, logins, or profiles.
  • Feeds, followers, likes, or comments.
  • Push notifications, streaks, or guilt.
  • Ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • AI that writes or designs your zine for you.
  • Anything that needs our servers to keep your work.

Looking for changes you can already use? See what’s new. Missing something that would help you make a zine? Tell us through our optional Google Form. How we handle feedback.
