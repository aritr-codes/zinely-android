Foundation

Getting Zinely, keeping your work

The ground under the desk

  • Available Works offline with no account. Back up your whole shelf to a file and restore it later.
  • Available 0.9.0-beta.6, backups that tell you what happened. Backups tells you when you last backed up. A backup that fails says where it failed, and one zine that can’t be read no longer stops the rest. Restoring adds only the zines that aren’t already on your shelf. See what changed.
  • In development Folders on your shelf. Make your own folders to keep zines together. This is at the design stage, so there is nothing to try yet.
  • Planned Zinely on Google Play. Account approval, testing, and store review still have to happen. Until then, download the Android beta here.

Craft

Making the pages

On the bench

  • Available Photos, words, and Art. Move a photo inside its frame, flip, copy, the photocopier look, undo and redo.
  • Available New in beta.6. Undo and redo say what they did. A tap in the hole of a window frame, a ring, or a photo corner reaches what’s underneath. With a screen reader, a page reads top to bottom. The Font button that did nothing is gone.
  • In development A second font for your words. Book, a bookish style, beside today’s plain one. This is at the design stage, so there is nothing to try yet.
  • Planned Two hand-cut frames for your pages.
  • Planned Page backgrounds. Something behind your photos and words other than plain paper.
  • Planned Photo descriptions. Add a few words to a photo so a screen reader can say what it shows when the zine is read in the app. Printed and PDF copies won’t carry it.
  • Exploring More Art, see-through photos, and new ways to crop or cut out photos. These are ideas under consideration, not announced features.

Proof

Reading, printing, folding

From screen to paper

  • Available Read the finished zine page by page. Share a print-ready PDF for A4 or US Letter paper, or save it to Downloads (on Android 7 to 9, Zinely asks for permission first). Follow the fold guide in the app or on this site.
  • Planned Help at the printer. Clearer guidance at the print step, so you can check one sheet came out right before you print the rest.
  • Planned More paper sizes. Today Zinely prints on A4 and US Letter.
  • Exploring Fewer “wait, which way?” moments. We want to watch first-time makers use the fold guide with real paper, then improve the steps that make them pause.

Not on our list

Some things are off the table on purpose, so you never have to wonder:

  • Accounts, logins, or profiles.
  • Feeds, followers, likes, or comments.
  • Push notifications, streaks, or guilt.
  • Ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • AI that writes or designs your zine for you.
  • Anything that needs our servers to keep your work.

Looking for changes you can already use? See what’s new. Missing something that would help you make a zine? Tell us through our optional Google Form. How we handle feedback.
