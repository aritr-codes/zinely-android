Roadmap

What comes next.

A short list of what we intend to do and what we are still considering. Planned means we intend to deliver it; Exploring means no decision to build it yet. There are no promised release dates.

Last reviewed: 9 September 2026

Planned

Measure, then improve

  • Investigate Reframe loading and restore its regression test. Measure cold and repeated photo opening, and make the unavailable-photo accessibility test reliable. A speedup is not promised.
  • Publish on Google Play after developer-account verification, required testing, and store review. Account verification is currently blocked; the GitHub beta download is available now.

No additional app feature is committed for the next release yet.

Exploring

Small improvements worth testing

  • Remove the unavailable Font button. Test hiding the disabled Font action until font choice is supported.
  • Make the in-app fold guide clearer. Test the illustrated website guide with first-time makers before deciding whether the app needs new diagrams or optional motion.
