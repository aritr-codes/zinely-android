FoundationGetting Zinely, keeping your work

The ground under the desk

 6 Available1 Planned Open the listFold it away
  • Available Works offline. No account. Back up your whole shelf to a file and restore it later.
  • Available 0.9.0-beta.6: clearer backups. Backups shows when you last backed up. A failed backup says where it failed. One unreadable zine does not stop the rest. Restore adds only zines that are not on your shelf. See what changed.
  • Available 0.9.0-beta.7: folders on your shelf. Put zines in folders. A backup remembers each zine’s folder. See what changed.
  • Available New in beta.8: a note when Zinely repairs a zine’s details. If the file with a zine’s name, cover and folder is damaged, Zinely uses an earlier copy. Your shelf names the zine and keeps the note until you tap Got it. We have not yet listened to it with a screen reader. See what changed.
  • Available New in beta.8: fewer ways to lose work. Back keeps your words while you type. The Bench has a button back to your shelf. A failed Save PDF says why and says your zine is safe. We tried it on a phone and on emulators, not yet with a screen reader.
  • Available New in beta.8: select several zines. Move them to a folder or delete them together. One Undo brings them all back. We tried it on a phone and on emulators, not yet with a screen reader.
  • Planned Zinely on Google Play. Account approval, testing and store review must happen first. Until then, download the Android beta here.
CraftMaking the pages

On the bench

 7 Available5 Planned1 Exploring Open the listFold it away
  • Available Photos, words and Art. Move a photo inside its frame, flip, copy, the photocopier look, undo and redo.
  • Available New in beta.6. Undo and redo name the change. A tap in the hole of a window frame, ring or photo corner reaches what is under it. With a screen reader, a page reads top to bottom. The Font button that did nothing is gone.
  • Available New in beta.7: a second font. Book, a serif font, and Plain. Select words and tap Text to choose. We have not yet printed a page to check Book at its smallest size.
  • Available New in beta.8: two frames, Snipped border and Round window. We have not yet printed a page with them. We have not yet listened to them with a screen reader.
  • Available New in beta.8: the page no longer shrinks when you select something. One Arrange button holds the move, size, turn and layer buttons. We have not yet listened to it with a screen reader. We have not tried it on a real small phone.
  • Available New in beta.8: better Art, and more of it. A tester said some Art looked rough. We redrew it and added new pieces. There are 124 now, with shorter names. We tried it on a phone. We have not yet printed a page with the new pieces. We have not yet listened to them with a screen reader.
  • Available New in beta.8: mix your own ink. Pick any colour. My inks keeps the inks you mix. Save inks as a palette. A backup does not include your inks. We tried it on an emulator and a phone. We have not yet checked it on a larger phone. We have not yet listened to it with a screen reader.
  • Planned Paper colour, on screen. If you print on coloured paper, you can see your pages on that colour in the app. It shows on screen only. Zinely does not print a background colour. The colour comes from the paper you load.
  • Planned Photo descriptions. Add a few words to a photo. A screen reader says them when you read the zine in the app. Printed and PDF copies do not carry them. We designed what a screen reader says. We have not built it yet.
  • Planned A third font, Hand. It looks like handwriting. It joins Book and Plain.
  • Planned Your own drawings. Photograph a drawing and put it on the page in one ink, like the rest of the Art.
  • Planned Recent and favourite Art, kept after a restart. They stay when you open Zinely again.
  • Exploring See-through photos, new ways to crop photos, photos cut to a shape, and simple photo fixes such as black and white. These are ideas, not announced features. Some would undo choices we made on purpose. We will decide before we promise anything.
ProofReading, printing, folding

From screen to paper

 1 Available2 Planned1 Exploring Open the listFold it away
  • Available Read the finished zine page by page. Share a print-ready PDF for A4 or US Letter paper, or save it to Downloads (on Android 7 to 9, Zinely asks for permission first). Use the fold guide in the app or on this site.
  • Planned Help at the printer. Clearer help at the print step. You check one sheet before you print the rest.
  • Planned More paper sizes. Zinely now prints on A4 and US Letter. US Legal and A5 are next. We designed how you choose them. We have not built them yet.
  • Exploring Easier folding. We want to watch first-time makers use the fold guide with real paper. Then we will improve the steps that make them stop.

Shipped, but not finished

Some things are in the download before we finish checking them. This list shows what we still owe and what we know is wrong. The download page has the main ones.

  • The Book font: we have not yet printed a page to check it at its smallest size.
  • Folders, the second font, tap-through, select several zines, mixed inks, the new Art and the fixes to Back and Save PDF: not yet checked by ear with a screen reader.
  • The new Art pieces and the two frames: we have not printed a page yet.
  • Restore on a different phone: not yet tried.
  • Save PDF on Android 7 to 9: checked on an Android 9 emulator, not on a real Android 7, 8 or 9 phone.
  • Known problem: at the largest text size, the text controls crowd together. The word “Right” breaks across lines. Fixed in beta.8.
  • Known problem: while you type, a notice can say a character “can’t print yet” when it does print. Fixed in beta.8.
  • Known problem, seen once with a file we damaged on purpose: when Zinely repairs a zine’s details from its spare copy, the zine can go back to an earlier title while the shelf shows the newer one. Your pages are not affected. Fixed in beta.8.
  • Known problem: at the largest text size on a small phone, the first-time tip can cut off its first and last words. Fixed in beta.8.
  • Known problem: at the largest text size on a small phone, the first-time tip can cover the piece you drag. Not fixed yet.
  • Known problem: at twice the normal text size, the “+ New palette” button in the Ink tray wraps onto two lines. Not fixed yet.
  • Known problem: if Zinely restores a zine’s main file from its backup copy, it does not tell you. The last few seconds of changes are lost. Not fixed yet.
  • A tester said the screen breaks in some places. In beta.8 we fixed many of them on small phones and with large text. We checked them on an emulator, not yet on a real small phone.

Not on our list

We decided not to do these things. You do not need to wonder.

  • Accounts, logins or profiles.
  • Feeds, followers, likes or comments.
  • Push notifications, streaks or guilt.
  • Ads, analytics or tracking.
  • AI that writes or designs your zine for you.
  • Anything that needs our servers to keep your work.

For changes you can use now, see what’s new. Do you need something we do not have? Tell us in our optional Google Form. How we handle feedback.
