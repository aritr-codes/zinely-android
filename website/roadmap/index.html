Our to-make list

What comes next.

Here’s where our attention is going. Some things are being tested. Others are still ideas scribbled in the margin. No release dates until we can stand behind them.

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Planned

An easier way to get Zinely

  • We want to bring Zinely to Google Play. Account approval, testing, and store review still have to happen. For now, you can download the Android beta here.

The current download is still 0.9.0-beta.4-r3. Work in progress does not update the app on your phone.

Exploring and testing

Less getting in your way

  • A tidier text toolbar. We’re checking a version without the Font button, because it doesn’t let you choose a font yet. This is still being tested, not in the download.
  • Fewer “wait, which way?” moments. We want to watch first-time makers use the fold guide with real paper, then improve the steps that make them pause.

Ideas, not promises

More ways to make it yours

We’re looking at font choices, stickers, frames, and new ways to crop or cut out photos. These are ideas under consideration, not announced features. We haven’t committed to building them yet.

Looking for changes you can already use? See what’s new. Missing something that would help you make a zine? Tell us through our optional Google Form, or email us. How we handle feedback.
