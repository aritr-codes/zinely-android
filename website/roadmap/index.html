FoundationGetting Zinely, keeping your work

The ground under the desk

 3 Available2 In development2 Planned Open the listFold it away
  • Available Works offline with no account. Back up your whole shelf to a file and restore it later.
  • Available 0.9.0-beta.6, backups that tell you what happened. Backups tells you when you last backed up. A backup that fails says where it failed, and one zine that can’t be read no longer stops the rest. Restoring adds only the zines that aren’t already on your shelf. See what changed.
  • Available 0.9.0-beta.7, folders on your shelf. Make your own folders to keep zines together. A folder shows as a pile of covers, and a backup remembers which folder each zine was in. See what changed.
  • In development A note when Zinely repairs a zine’s details. If the small file that holds a zine’s name, cover and folder is damaged, Zinely already uses its earlier copy. Your shelf will now say so, name the zine, and keep the note until you tap Got it. It is built. We still have to listen with a screen reader and try it on a real phone. There is nothing to try yet.
  • In development Fewer ways to lose work or get stuck. Pressing Back while you type keeps your words, the Bench gets a button back to your shelf, and when Save PDF fails, it says what went wrong. It is built, and we have tried it on a phone and on emulators. We still have to listen with a screen reader. There is nothing to try yet.
  • Planned Select several zines at once. Press and hold a zine, pick more, then move them to a folder or delete them together. One Undo brings the whole lot back. It is not designed or built yet.
  • Planned Zinely on Google Play. Account approval, testing, and store review still have to happen. Until then, download the Android beta here.
CraftMaking the pages

On the bench

 3 Available4 In development5 Planned1 Exploring Open the listFold it away
  • Available Photos, words, and Art. Move a photo inside its frame, flip, copy, the photocopier look, undo and redo.
  • Available New in beta.6. Undo and redo say what they did. A tap in the hole of a window frame, a ring, or a photo corner reaches what’s underneath. With a screen reader, a page reads top to bottom. The Font button that did nothing is gone.
  • Available New in beta.7: a second font for your words. Book, a bookish serif, beside today’s Plain. Select some words and tap Text to choose. We have not yet printed a page to check Book at its smallest size.
  • In development Two hand-cut frames for your pages, Snipped border and Round window. They are built. We still have to try them by hand, print a page, and listen with a screen reader. There is nothing to try yet.
  • In development Selecting something no longer shrinks the page. The buttons that move, size, turn and layer a piece now sit behind one Arrange button. It is built. We still have to listen with a screen reader and try it on a real small phone. There is nothing to try yet.
  • In development Better-drawn Art, and more of it. A tester told us some Art pieces look rough. We redrew the rough ones and chose new pieces to go with them: 124 in all, with shorter names so each name shows in full on its tile. It is built. We still have to try it on a phone, print a page, and listen with a screen reader. There is nothing to try yet.
  • In development Mix your own ink. Pick any colour, keep the ones you use in My inks, and save inks together as a palette. Backups won’t include your inks, so they won’t move to a new phone. We’re building it now. There is nothing to try yet.
  • Planned Paper colour, on screen. If you print on coloured paper, see your pages on that colour in the app. It is shown on screen only: Zinely won’t print a background colour, because the colour comes from the paper you load.
  • Planned Photo descriptions. Add a few words to a photo so a screen reader can say what it shows when the zine is read in the app. Printed and PDF copies won’t carry it. What a screen reader will say is designed; nothing is built yet.
  • Planned A third font, Hand, that looks written by hand, beside Book and Plain.
  • Planned Your own drawings. Photograph a drawing and use it on the page in one ink, like the rest of the Art.
  • Planned Your recent and favourite Art, kept after a restart. They will still be there the next time you open Zinely.
  • Exploring See-through photos, new ways to crop photos, and photos cut to a shape. Also simple photo fixes such as black and white. These are ideas under consideration, not announced features. Some would undo choices we made on purpose, so we are weighing them before we promise anything.
ProofReading, printing, folding

From screen to paper

 1 Available2 Planned1 Exploring Open the listFold it away
  • Available Read the finished zine page by page. Share a print-ready PDF for A4 or US Letter paper, or save it to Downloads (on Android 7 to 9, Zinely asks for permission first). Follow the fold guide in the app or on this site.
  • Planned Help at the printer. Clearer guidance at the print step, so you can check one sheet came out right before you print the rest.
  • Planned More paper sizes. Today Zinely prints on A4 and US Letter. US Legal and A5 come next. How you’ll choose them is designed; they aren’t built yet.
  • Exploring Fewer “wait, which way?” moments. We want to watch first-time makers use the fold guide with real paper, then improve the steps that make them pause.

Shipped, but not finished

Some things are in the download before we have finished checking them. Here is some of what we still owe, and what we know is wrong. The download page keeps the main ones.

  • The Book font: we have not yet printed a page to check it at its smallest size.
  • Folders, the second font, and tapping through a frame: not yet checked by ear with a screen reader.
  • Restoring a backup onto a different phone: not yet tried.
  • Save PDF on Android 7 to 9: checked on an Android 9 emulator, not on a real Android 7, 8 or 9 phone.
  • Known problem: at the largest text size the text controls crowd together, and the word “Right” breaks across lines. Fixed for the next download.
  • Known problem: while you type, a notice can say a character “can’t print yet” when it does print. Fixed for the next download.
  • Known problem, seen once with a file we damaged on purpose: when Zinely repairs a zine’s damaged details from its spare copy, the zine can go back to an earlier title while the shelf still shows the newer one. Your pages are not affected. Fixed for the next download.
  • Known problem: at the largest text size on a small phone, the tip shown the first time you place something can cut off its first and last words. Fixed for the next download.
  • Known problem: at the largest text size on a small phone, the first-time tip can cover the piece it asks you to drag. Not fixed yet.
  • A tester told us the screen breaks in places. We found and fixed many of them on small phones and with large text, for the next download. We have checked them on an emulator, not yet on a real small phone.

Not on our list

Some things are off the table on purpose, so you never have to wonder:

  • Accounts, logins, or profiles.
  • Feeds, followers, likes, or comments.
  • Push notifications, streaks, or guilt.
  • Ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • AI that writes or designs your zine for you.
  • Anything that needs our servers to keep your work.

Looking for changes you can already use? See what’s new. Missing something that would help you make a zine? Tell us through our optional Google Form. How we handle feedback.
