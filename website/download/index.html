Get the Android beta.

Zinely is free and in public beta. You install it straight from GitHub rather than a store, which takes a few more taps. This page walks you through them.

Install it

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download the APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps, then go back.
  3. Tap Install, then open Zinely.

Android may show a warning because the app did not come from a store. That is normal for a direct download. Only install Zinely from this page or from the project’s GitHub releases.

Updating from an earlier beta

A newer beta installs over the one you have and keeps your zines. Make a backup first anyway: on your shelf, open Backups and choose Back up this shelf, then keep the file somewhere you control.

Using Zinely 0.8.0 or older? Those early builds cannot update to the beta. Share anything you care about somewhere safe first, then uninstall the old app and install this one. Uninstalling deletes the old app’s zines.

Moving to a new phone

Zinely has no account and no cloud, so nothing follows you to a new phone on its own. Android’s backup and phone-transfer tools do not copy your zines either. To bring them with you:

  1. On the old phone, open Backups and choose Back up this shelf.
  2. Move the backup file to the new phone however you like, for example by cable or through your own cloud storage.
  3. Install Zinely on the new phone, open Backups, and choose Restore a backup.

Keep the backup file until you have opened your zines on the new phone. Restoring on a different phone has not been tested yet, so check your zines there before you wipe the old phone, and tell us if anything looks wrong. Uninstalling Zinely deletes the zines inside it.

Known limits in this version

  • On Android 7, 8 and 9, Save PDF does not work in this beta. Use Share instead and send the PDF to your Files app, email, or a printer app. Android 10 and newer can save normally.
  • A flipped photo sent across the fold prints with the spread broken at the fold.
  • Pictures with see-through backgrounds, like logos, come in with black where they should be clear.
  • Text in scripts Zinely cannot print yet, such as Bengali or Tamil, is kept and flagged, but does not print.
  • Zinely makes the PDF. Printing happens in your phone’s PDF or print app.

When the next beta is released, what’s new will say which of these it fixes.
