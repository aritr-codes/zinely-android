Get Zinely for Android.

Download the APK

The file is zinely-1.0.0-release.apk. For the changes in this version, read what’s new or the release page on GitHub.

Install it

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download the APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, let your browser or Files app install unknown apps. Then go back.
  3. Tap Install. Then open Zinely.

Android can show a warning because the app did not come from a store. This is normal for a direct download. Install Zinely only from this page or from the GitHub releases.

Updating from a beta

Zinely 1.0.0 installs over the beta you have. It keeps your zines. It has the same Zinely release key (certificate SHA-256 8ade47cf70c18b5e6f4901c89fdab97d40a2d17e4c5acf33e0e1f0b6de469b36), so Android sees it as an update. Do not uninstall the beta first. Uninstalling deletes your zines. Make a backup first. On your shelf, open Backups and tap Back up this shelf. Keep the file in a safe place.

Do you use Zinely 0.8.0 or older? Those early builds cannot update to this version. Share your zines first. Then uninstall the old app and install this one. Uninstalling deletes the old app’s zines.

Moving to a new phone

Zinely has no account and no cloud. Your zines do not move to a new phone by themselves. Android’s backup and phone-transfer tools do not copy them. To move them:

  1. On the old phone, open Backups and choose Back up this shelf.
  2. Move the backup file to the new phone. Use a cable or your own cloud storage.
  3. Install Zinely on the new phone. Open Backups and tap Restore a backup.

Keep the backup file until your zines open on the new phone. We have not yet tested a restore on a different phone. Check your zines before you erase the old phone. Tell us if something looks wrong. Uninstalling Zinely deletes the zines inside it.

Known limits in this version

  • We have not tested the new features with a screen reader. Nothing new in this version has been tested that way. This covers Hand, Describe, what Read now says, US Legal and A5 paper, and the Start sheet and paper colour sheet. It also covers the turn knob, Straighten, turning with two fingers, small pieces, the lighter ring and the new Cancel button. It also covers the older features from the last version: select several zines, mixed inks, the new Art and frames, and the changes to Back and Save PDF. We tried them on a phone and on emulators. We read what the phone reports. We did not listen to it.
  • The turn knob. Over the dark area beside the page, the line from the knob to the piece is hard to see. The knob itself stays easy to see. Its touch area can take a tap meant for what is under it. We have not tried adding a second finger late, after a long press has cleared your choice.
  • We have not yet printed a page with the new Art pieces. We have not checked mixed inks on a larger phone. At twice the normal text size, “+ New palette” wraps onto two lines.
  • At the largest text size on a small phone, the notice for a change that cannot be saved can be taller than the screen. At twice the normal text size, the two buttons on the folder sheet stack one above the other.
  • If Zinely restores a zine’s main file from its backup copy, it does not tell you. The last few seconds of changes are lost.
  • Nobody has printed on coloured paper and compared it with the screen. Zinely shows paper colour on screen only. It never prints the colour and never puts it in the PDF. You put the coloured paper in your printer. The colours on screen are close estimates, and the sheet says so.
  • On a phone 360 dp wide and 640 dp tall, the paper colour sheet scrolls about 16 dp, so the last line of its foot sits at the screen edge until you scroll. A taller phone does not scroll. At twice the normal text size the sheet scrolls on the phones we tried, and Done stays on the screen.
  • We tried Android 7 to 9 on an emulator, not on a real phone.
  • The paper colour sheet closes with Done, Back or a tap outside it. It does not close with a swipe down. No Zinely sheet does.
  • A backup with a described photo or Art piece, or with a US Legal or A5 zine, restores only in Zinely 1.0.0 or newer. An older Zinely says the backup needs a newer Zinely and restores nothing from it. Older versions do not keep a zine’s paper colour.
  • The two new frames, Snipped border and Round window. Snipped border is thin. Over a photo, it can be hard to pick up again. A frame that is not square has a band that is thicker on two sides. We have not checked tapping through a frame with a screen reader.
  • If you go back to an older version, a zine made with Hand shows in a plainer font. Your words and your choice are kept, and Hand comes back in this version. Some words, a title in capitals for one, may not show on the older version. A zine with a new Art piece opens, but the piece is not drawn. An invisible frame can still be selected, and it takes a tap meant for what is under it. A backup does not include your mixed inks and palettes.
  • Folders, the Book font and the Hand font are not fully checked. We ran them on one phone. We have not tested them with a screen reader. We have not yet printed a page to check Book at 12 pt, its smallest size.
  • Words that just fit their box can lose their last line when you switch to Book. There is no warning. The two fonts have different sizes and the box does not grow. Make the box taller to see the line again.
  • If you go back to an older version of Zinely, or restore a backup there, Book words show in Plain. No words are lost. They are Book again in this version. In the older version, lines that do not fit their box are missing from the page and the PDF. It does not tell you.
  • An older version of Zinely restores a backup with every zine on My Shelf, not in its folder. If the phone already has the same zines, a restore adds nothing. It brings no folders.
  • Book has no Greek or Cyrillic letters. You cannot choose it for words smaller than 12 pt. The card tells you why.
  • With large words, each line can still move by about a pixel when you tap Done.
  • When you select words, two buttons open the same card: Text, above the page strip, and Text style, in the row of arrows at the bottom.
  • With TalkBack, the reading position does not move to the result after you move a zine or change a folder. If you tap a second folder action while the first is still saving, Zinely drops the second one with no message.
  • When the last zine leaves a folder, the message and its Undo stay for four seconds. This is short for two lines.
  • We have not yet tried a first-time install of this file. Make a backup before you update.
  • On Android 7, 8 and 9, Save PDF asks for storage access. If you decline and tell Android not to ask again, Save PDF shows “Couldn’t make the PDF” until you allow storage for Zinely in your phone’s settings. Share always works. We tested this on an Android 9 emulator, but not yet on a real Android 7, 8 or 9 phone. If you have one, tell us how it goes.
  • Pictures with see-through backgrounds, such as logos, lose their transparency. The clear parts come in as white. They print correctly on white paper. On the cream page in the app, a logo shows as a faint white rectangle. Over a photo, it shows as a white box.
  • With TalkBack, About and its credits pages open on the Back button. Going back lands at the top of the previous screen. You can still reach everything by swiping.
  • Tapping through a frame is new. A tap in the empty part of a window frame, ring, eyelet, torn hole, photo corner or registration cross reaches what is under it. We have not yet checked it by ear with TalkBack. A thin frame over a photo can need a careful tap. Dragging a selected frame by its edge resizes it. Drag from the middle to move it.
  • On a narrow piece, the resize handles reach a little beyond its edge. A drag there resizes the piece. A tap very close to a handle keeps your choice.
  • Your recent and favourite Art pieces stay on the phone. A backup does not include them.
  • With TalkBack, if undo says the same line twice in a row, you may hear it only once.
  • If a backup left a zine out, this version says so when you restore it. Earlier versions do not say it.
  • Photos you replace or delete still use space in the app. Zinely does not free that space yet.
  • Zinely cannot print some scripts yet, such as Bengali and Tamil. It keeps and flags the text, but the text does not print.
  • Zinely makes the PDF. Your phone’s PDF or print app does the printing.
